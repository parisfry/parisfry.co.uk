My creative practice is inspired by visual culture, design, and immersive experiences. I enjoy exploring how print, creative coding and digital technologies can come together to create engaging visual experiences.

I studied Textile Design at Chelsea College of Arts alongside a Diploma in Creative Computing, graduating with First Class Honours. Beginning with print design, my practice has evolved to include generative design and digital experiences, allowing me to combine traditional design principles with digital experimentation.

Alongside my creative work, I’ve developed experience in content creation and digital marketing through producing social media content and building online communities.
