DIGITAL CAMPAIGN CONCEPT FOR 'SUGAR GIRL' EP

Creative marketing proposal developed in response to a live recruitment brief.

Sugar Girl is a digital campaign concept created in response to a creative marketing brief for Little Simz's EP release. The project explores how social-first content, platform trends, and audience engagement can be combined to create a campaign that feels authentic to both the artist and her audience.

PROJECT BRIEF

Develop a digital marketing campaign for Little Simz's Sugar Girl EP, creating engaging content that builds anticipation, encourages audience interaction and extends the life of the project across social platforms.

CONTENT PROPOSAL - INTERACTIVE CAMPAIGN MOCKUP

CONCEPT:

Interactive carousel inspired by nostalgic magazine personality quizzes. Users answer a series of questions by swiping through the slides to reveal which track from Sugar Girl best matches their personality.

PURPOSE:

  • Increases engagement through swipeable content
  • Encourages interaction — saves and shares
  • Helps generate conversation as users discuss their results and compare with other fans
  • Promotes all songs on the EP, with the potential to include an unreleased track and pre-save option

FORMAT:

Ideal platform would be an Instagram carousel, with the potential to be adapted to TikTok or an Instagram Story poll.

CONTENT PROPOSAL - ARTIST-LED CONTENT

CONCEPT:

A short form series where Little Simz rates some of her favourite, funniest, or most unexpected lyrics from Sugar Girl.

  • Lyrics are presented on screen
  • Little Simz reacts and gives them a rating out of 10
  • She explains the meaning and story behind the lyrics
  • Repeat for 3–4 different lyrics

PURPOSE:

Give fans a more personal way into the lyrics while creating native, personality-led content that encourages comments and sharing.

FORMAT:

Instagram reels/tiktoks, 15-30 seconds, 2-4 lyrics per video, artist-led

CONTENT PRODUCTION - SHORT FORM VIDEO ASSETS

CONCEPT:

  • Fast-paced promotional edit
  • Bold typography
  • Beat synchronised editing
  • Short duration and attention grabbing with the intention to build excitement

PLATFORM / USAGE:

Ideal for Tiktok and Instagram Reels as quick pacing allows for repeat viewing, and strong visuals increase retention.

Meta Ad, artist’s socials, campaign teaser.

  • Uses Spotify as a familiar interface to hook viewers
  • Recreates the experience of discovering the EP on Spotify
  • A brief preview of each track encouraging listeners to listen in full

PLATFORM / USAGE:

Ideal for Reels, Tiktok, or Youtube Shorts as it is music led content.

CONTENT PRODUCTION - STATIC SOCIAL ASSET

CONCEPT:

A striking still from the campaign, combined with bold typography and a clear CTA to drive streams amoung new audiences. A simple and effective asset.

PURPOSE:

  • A flexible campaign asset designed to announce and promote the EP.
  • Key branding makes it instantly recognisable in-feed and easy to adapt across organic and paid content.
  • Used to target users who follow similar artists or engage with similar music genres.

FORMAT:

Paid Meta ADs (Instagram & Facebook), Little Simz sponsored posts, Music publication social accounts e.g. Dazed, NME
