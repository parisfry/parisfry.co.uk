ABOUT

I am a London-based multidisciplinary artist combining printmaking and creative coding, exploring the intersection of digital design and immersive environments.

I studied Textile Design at Chelsea College of Arts, UAL, alongside a diploma in Creative Computing, where I learned programmes such as P5.js and TouchDesigner. These digital tools heavily informed my final-year projects and continue to shape my approach to making.

My work combines traditional print processes with computational design methods to explore the relationship between analogue and digital image-making.

Influenced by rhythm, repetition, and the visual language of nightlife and rave culture, I use my practice as a space for experimentation across physical and digital mediums.
